Key takeaways:
- Moderate signal on primary correctness/rubric-coverage (kappa 0.500-0.583), not yet calibration-grade for fine-grained pass-rate claims: 66.7% exact agreement (20/30), tilting slightly lenient (6 lenient vs 4 strict).
- Blind spot on rendered multi-part replies and withheld explanations (y008, x005, x010, x021): on y008 Gemma read the bottom disclaimer and missed top-of-reply findings telling an M-1 student to file OPT and giving a 30-day departure period.
